One-line joke about a meeting that should have been an email, for make.fun's "The Meeting That Should Have Been an Email" bounty ($2 in USDC). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes the entry to `<OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt` and validates it against the gate before exiting.

## Entry

> "Halfway through the meeting, the email the meeting was about finally arrived."

- Characters: 77 (counted per code point)
- Bytes: 77 (UTF-8)
- Lines: 1, no trailing newline

## Why this line

Angles considered: a meeting whose only outcome is scheduling another meeting; a "quick sync" that balloons; a meeting to discuss why there are too many meetings; the mute button; and the email arriving *during* the meeting. The last one won because it is the freshest and most economical: it never says "could have been an email" — it shows it. The email that could have replaced the meeting turns up halfway through the meeting itself, so the punch lands on the final word ("arrived") and the joke stands alone with no setup. It is also instantly recognisable: anyone who has sat in that meeting has watched the very email being discussed land in their inbox mid-meeting. Every word earns its place — 77 characters, no throat-clearing, no second sentence.

## Run it

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/meeting-email-out ~/workspace/makefun/node22/bin/node main.ts
```
